Welcome! I'm Van Tran, a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Chicago, advised by Prof. Nick Feamster in the NOISE Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, cybersecurity, and privacy, with a focus on leveraging AI to address real-world security and privacy challenges while making AI systems themselves more secure and trustworthy.

Research Interests

  • AI for Security and Privacy: Developing scalable, ML-driven techniques to detect cybersecurity threats, Internet censorship, and violations of privacy regulations, including the CCPA.
  • Security and Privacy of Generative AI: Investigating privacy vulnerabilities in generative models, including membership inference, data extraction, and sensitive information leakage, and evaluating the effectiveness of privacy defenses.
  • AI-Driven Cyber Threats: Measuring and characterizing the growing use of LLMs in malicious activities, particularly phishing and spam.
  • Secure and Privacy-Preserving AI Agents: Designing fine-grained authorization and access control mechanisms to protect sensitive resources and prevent unauthorized actions in autonomous AI systems, as well as context-aware privacy safeguards to prevent sensitive information leakage in AI agents.
